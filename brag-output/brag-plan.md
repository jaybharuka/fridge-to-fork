# Fridge to Fork — brag plan

**Angle:** The whole point of the app in one line: it doesn't just give you a recipe, it tells you exactly what's missing and lets Swiggy bring it. Show the real product doing that, start to finish, in one unbroken scroll-through.

**Hook:** Cold open on the real dark-mode UI: "Cook anything. Order what's missing." — the actual hero headline, no invented copy.

**Highlights (from the real product):**
1. Type a dish → orange "Get Recipe" button (the exact CSS shimmer button)
2. Real result screen: "13 of 19 ingredients · 6 to order", pantry staples struck through, checklist ticking
3. The payoff: "How do you want to handle this?" → two real un-ranked choice cards, "Powered by Swiggy"

**Punchline:** Wordmark + tagline + "github.com/jaybharuka/fridge-to-fork"

**Tone:** default (punchy, playful, clean) — matches a solo-dev kitchen-assistant launch, not corporate SaaS.

**Visual identity:** exact site tokens — bg #0F0F0F, card #1E1E1E, orange #FC8019, text #F5F5F5, Plus Jakarta Sans. Dark mode (matches what the real browser rendered).

**Format:** vertical 1080x1920 — this is a mobile web product, phone-shaped chrome makes the flow instantly legible. 20s, 30fps.

## Storyboard (20s)

| # | Time | Scene | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–2.5s | Black → phone frame fades up on the real home screen, cursor types "Paneer Tikka" into the real input | typing simulation, autocomplete pill pops in |
| 2 | 2.5–4.5s | Tap "Get Recipe" (shimmer flash), quick zoom-punch transition | button press + flash cut |
| 3 | 4.5–9.5s | Real results screen: hero photo, "13 of 19 ingredients · 6 to order", checklist rows ticking on one by one (Paneer, Yogurt, Besan unchecked; Ginger-Garlic/Onion/Tomato staple-struck) | staggered row reveal, staple badges pulse in |
| 4 | 9.5–14.5s | Cut to the decision card: "How do you want to handle this?" — both choice cards slide up, "Powered by Swiggy" settles | slide-up + hold (readable) |
| 5 | 14.5–17.5s | Snap zoom to orange "Order missing items from Instamart" card, cart icon bounces, checkmark burst | punch-in, bounce |
| 6 | 17.5–20s | Wordmark + tagline outro: fork/knife mark, "Fridge to Fork", "Cook anything. Order what's missing.", "github.com/jaybharuka/fridge-to-fork" | mark draws in, text settles |

## Sound
Minimal synthesized layer (no licensed track available on this machine): soft rising pad under scenes 1-2, a light "tick" blip per checklist row in scene 3, a warm confirm chime under scene 5, low pad fade in outro. Kept low and non-harsh — texture, not a beat-drop.
